PRIVACY POLICY

Gengrail TCG Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains how Gengrail TCG handles information when you connect an eBay account to Gengrail services or use Gengrail's inventory, sales and fulfilment tools.

2. Information we may process

When you connect eBay, Gengrail may process information made available through eBay's APIs, including your eBay user identifier, listing information, order information, fulfilment status and other seller-account data required to provide the integration.

3. How we use information

We use this information only to provide Gengrail functionality, such as creating or managing listings, synchronising sales and orders, supporting picking, packing and dispatch, and maintaining business records inside the Gengrail system.

4. eBay authentication

Gengrail uses eBay's OAuth process to request permission to access eBay account data. Gengrail should not ask for or store your eBay password. Access is granted through eBay and can be revoked through eBay's account or developer controls.

5. Storage and security

Authentication secrets and long-lived access credentials should be stored securely on server-side infrastructure and not embedded in publicly accessible browser code. Gengrail aims to limit access to data to what is necessary for the service to function.

6. Sharing

Gengrail does not sell eBay account data. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the application or integration, where necessary to provide the service.

7. Retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain business records, meet legal or accounting obligations, or resolve disputes.

8. Your choices

You may disconnect eBay access at any time. Where technically applicable, you may also request deletion or correction of personal information held by Gengrail.

9. Contact

For privacy or data questions, contact Gengrail TCG using the current business contact details published by Gengrail.

This page is intended as a practical privacy notice for the current Gengrail TCG integration and should be reviewed again before any public commercial launch.
